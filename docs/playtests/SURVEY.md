# Playtest survey

About 5 minutes per player, right after the run. Ratings are 1 (strongly disagree) to 5
(strongly agree). Each "one line" answer is optional but is the best evidence for a change.
The questions follow the game's config (`pnpm sim -- kit`).

<!-- kit:questions -->
| # | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The trip out to gather and back to the base felt **tense** (not tedious). | 1-5 + one line |
| 2 | The 10-card deck with a hand of 5 gave a good number of turns in each phase. | 1-5 + one line |
| 3 | Stepping off the edge to reveal a tile was a real choice: I weighed the new enemies against what I could gather. | 1-5 + one line |
| 4 | Single-use gathering nodes pushed me out to new tiles at a good pace. | 1-5 + one line |
| 5 | Engagements were a good gamble: I chose where to engage from, and the enemy dice felt fair. | 1-5 + one line |
| 6 | Which Skill felt best? Which felt useless? | one line each |
| 7 | Rolling and keeping dice was fun, not fiddly. | 1-5 |
| 8 | I always knew what I could do next. | 1-5 + the moment you did not |
| 9 | A knockout felt like a setback I could recover from, not the end. | 1-5 + one line |
| 10 | Which rule confused you, and when? | one line |
| 11 | The run was the right length. | 1-5 |
| 12 | Co-op only: turns moved at a good pace, and I had real choices on other players' turns. | 1-5 + one line |
| 13 | I would play again tomorrow. | 1-5 |
| 14 | The one thing you would change. | one line |
<!-- /kit:questions -->
