Survival Dice‑Builder

An open-world survival game on Mage Knight-style hex tiles. A deck of dual-use cards drives building and combat. A growing pool of custom d6 resolves Yahtzee-style Skills. You cannot win. You stay alive as long as possible.

Written to ASD-STE100 writing rules

01Round loop

5.1Each round has 3 phases in this sequence: Prepare, Combat, Explore.
5.3Each round uses 2 passes through your deck.
5.4Your deck can grow. Each new card makes Prepare longer and Combat longer.

02Map and sites

3.1Each map tile has 7 hexes: 1 center hex and 6 outer hexes.
3.2A countryside tile has a green back. A core tile has a brown back.
3.4Figures and enemies cannot enter a lake hex or a mountain hex.
6.7Build: Pay the materials on the reference card. Turn the broken location to its built side.

04Dice and Skills

2.4The Star face is wild. A Star counts as any one face of your choice.
7.7.3Keep the dice that you want. Roll the other dice again.
7.7.6Put dice on your Skills. Use each die 1 time only. Use each Skill 1 time only.

05Enemies

9.2A spawn node gets a new enemy only when its enemy is defeated.
7.4Each enemy moves 2 hexes toward the nearest player or built location.
6.11Enemies are obstacles in the Prepare phase.
10.1Siege: Each enemy next to a built location gives 1 damage to that location.
6.12Skirmish: If you move into a hex with an enemy, a skirmish starts.

06Experience

8.1Each defeated enemy gives experience to all players.
8.4At each new level, each player gets 1 action die. There is no maximum.
8.5Each threshold is higher than the last.
