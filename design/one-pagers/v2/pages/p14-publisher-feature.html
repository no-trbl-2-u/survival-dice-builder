ONE DECK, TWO FACES: top halves up to walk out and build; flipped, to hold the line with dice.

The loop

Key beats

  1. Prepare Draw 3, play top halves: Move, Gather (node then spent), Build, Rest.
  2. Prepare Step off the edge for 1: a tile lands underfoot; keep walking.
  3. Combat Enemies walk up to 2 hexes toward the nearest structure; every spawn node spawns.
  4. Combat Hand of 3: roll, keep, reroll (up to 3 rolls); dice onto Skills; enemies hunting you hit. Deck empty: each enemy not next to a player hits an adjacent structure once.
  5. Round end Discards turn over as the next deck; round +1; fallen figures return.

Three ways to play

  • Turtle: never leave the Base tile; Build only upgrades.
  • Wall-builder: Barricades (2) and Towers (4) take the hits, not people.
  • Lure: stand 2 or more hexes nearer than any structure; levels for everyone.

Intended feel

Prepare Appetite. A quiet map; every edge is a tile.

Combat Siege. Feet planted; the base takes what you miss.

Round end Audit. Milestones, a bigger deck, one more round.
